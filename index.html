About the building

Set on Al Zahra'a Street in the Al Nakhil district, Al Barook Building is a ten-storey residential address built for tenants who want to move in without buying a single piece of furniture. Every one of its 80 flats is fully furnished and ready to live in from day one.

The building sits close to Al Ihsan Charity Association in Al Rumailah 1, within easy reach of Ajman's everyday essentials, with on-site management that keeps plumbing, electrical, carpentry and painting work handled promptly.

Our commitment to excellence means your comfort and satisfaction are our priority. From maintenance to security, we handle every detail so you can focus on settling into your new home.
