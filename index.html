About Al Barook Building

Al Barook Building stands as a beacon of modern residential comfort in Al Rumailah 1, Ajman. Set on Al Zahra'a Street in the serene Al Nakhil district, this 10-storey residential landmark offers 80 fully furnished apartments designed for those who value quality and convenience.

Every unit comes ready to live in from day one—no furniture shopping, no setup delays. Our commitment extends beyond providing apartments; we ensure a lifestyle where comfort meets professionalism.

From prompt maintenance services to 24/7 security surveillance, from central air conditioning to dedicated parking, every detail has been carefully considered for your peace of mind.
